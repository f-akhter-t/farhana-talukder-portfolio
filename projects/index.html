Dual-Source RAG Calibration

An undergraduate thesis studying how combining medical literature with patient-case evidence affects both the accuracy and the trustworthiness of AI-generated answers in clinical question-answering. Runs controlled experiments across three biomedical datasets to show when a second evidence source helps, when it does not, and why.

Bangla Disaster Severity

A five-class severity classifier for Bangla disaster posts on social media, built with team RaspberryPies for the IIUC CSE Fest 2026 datathon. Ablations showed text alone beat image and fusion models, and the final fine-tuned mDeBERTa-v3 pipeline reached 0.765 weighted F1 and 9th place.

ProjectPython

Image Reconstruction

A classical, non-ML pipeline that fills corrupted image regions using fast interpolation, PDE-based harmonic inpainting and Krylov solvers. Across 15 test images, Conjugate Gradient methods gave the sharpest results in about 62 iterations, against about 710 for Jacobi.
